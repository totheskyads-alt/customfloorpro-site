Cookie Policy.

Which cookies this website uses, what each one does, and how to change your choice at any time.

Last updated: September 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Some are needed for the site to work at all; others are optional and only run if you allow them.

Your choice comes first

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept optional cookies. Until you accept, no analytics or marketing cookies run in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. You can change your decision whenever you like using the Cookie settings link, which is also in the footer of every page.

Turning cookies off in your browser

Every browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking all cookies may stop parts of this site from working properly.
